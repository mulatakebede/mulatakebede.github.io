Ge'ez Language Part-of-Speech Tagger

Developed the first annotated corpus for Ge'ez — one of Africa's oldest languages — manually tagging 15,154 words from 1,305 sentences. Built a novel hybrid TnT + rule-based + regex POS tagger achieving 94.32% accuracy, significantly outperforming baseline models. Published in AAU ETD Repository.
