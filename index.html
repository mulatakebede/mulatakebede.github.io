Ge'ez Language Part-of-Speech Tagger

Built the first annotated corpus for Ge'ez — one of Africa's oldest languages — manually tagging 15,154 words from 1,305 sentences with 26 tag sets. Developed a hybrid TnT + rule-based + regex POS tagger achieving 94.32% accuracy. Published in AAU ETD Repository.
